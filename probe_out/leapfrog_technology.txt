Your application
journey
Our hiring process is designed to ensure that both you and Leapfrog are the perfect match for each other. While our process may vary depending on the role, here's an overview of what you can expect when you apply to join our team.
Step
1
Apply online
Begin your journey by submitting your application online. We encourage you to choose positions that truly excite you and align with your skills and aspirations.
Step
2
Profile screening
Once we receive your application, our dedicated talent acquisition team will review your resume, portfolio (if applicable), and evaluate your qualifications. This initial screening helps us identify candidates who closely align with the role's requirements.
Step
3
Screening call
If your qualifications meet our criteria, you'll receive a scheduled screening call. This is an opportunity for us to learn more about your background, experiences, and career aspirations. It's also a chance for you to get to know Leapfrog better.
Step
4
Technical assessment or written assignment
Depending on the role, you may be asked to complete an assessment. This helps us gauge your specific skills and knowledge related to the position.
Step
5
Face-to-face virtual or onsite interview
For some roles, we'll invite you for an interview, which can be conducted virtually or onsite. This interview allows us to dive deeper into your capabilities and assess your cultural fit with Leapfrog.
Step
6
Aligning expectations
We believe in open and honest communication. During this phase, we'll discuss your expectations and the role's responsibilities to ensure that both parties are on the same page.
Step
7
Reference check
We may conduct reference checks to gain insights into your past performance and character, helping us make a well-informed hiring decision.
Step
8
HR interview
Our HR team will have a conversation with you to discuss compensation, benefits, and other important details. We want you to have a clear understanding of what it means to be part of the Leapfrog family.
Step
9
Offer
Congratulations! If all goes well, we'll extend an offer for you to join our team. We'll provide guidance on the next steps, including the onboarding process.
Step
10
Onboarding
As you embark on your journey at Leapfrog, our people management team will ensure a smooth transition. We'll walk you through everything you need to know to start your new role.
